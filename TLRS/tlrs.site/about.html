About TLRS

A Team of Professional
Visual Scientists

The Living Room Studios started from a simple idea: the best photography feels less like a transaction and more like being hosted somewhere comfortable.

We call ourselves visual scientists because that's really the job — testing light, angles and timing with precision, then trusting instinct for the moment that actually matters. Every shoot, whether it's a wedding, a campaign or a single headshot, gets that same care.
